Letting Meta lead the charge on superintelligence, huh? It's a good thing they have a responsible and trustworthy track record, right? Well, think again:

**2025:**

- Meta secretly tracked Android users' browsing through hidden methods and settled a $1.4 billion lawsuit in Texas over biometric data collection without consent.

**2024:**

- The EU investigated Meta for issues related to child safety, false advertising, and data privacy.
- Forty-two U.S. states sued Meta for utilizing addictive algorithms that harm minors.
- The FTC accused Meta of breaching privacy rules, particularly concerning teenagers.

**2023:**

- Fined €1.2 billion by Ireland for illegal data transfers to the U.S. (GDPR violation).
- Health data leaks were revealed through the Meta Pixel.
- Fined €405 million for inadequate data protection for children on Instagram.
- Fined €390 million for enforcing personalized ads.

**2022:**

- A data breach exposed information of 533 million users, resulting in a €265 million fine.
- Settled for $725 million over the secret sharing of user data, including with Cambridge Analytica.

**2021:**

- Paid $650 million to settle claims regarding facial recognition privacy violations.

**2019:**

- The FTC fined Meta $5 billion for privacy violations, and the SEC $100 million for misleading investors.

**2018:**

- Cambridge Analytica misused data from up to 87 million users.
- A bug exposed details of 29 million users, leading to a €251 million fine.
- Hackers compromised 90 million accounts due to a vulnerability.

**2013–2018:**

- Meta shared user data with over 150 companies without clear consent.
- Early bugs exposed millions of users' contact information.

**Ongoing Issues:**

- Tracks non-users through "shadow profiles."
- Records location information even when it's disabled.
- WhatsApp privacy policy changes triggered backlash.
- Facebook conducted non-consensual psychological experiments.

It's important to remember that Big Tech prioritizes business, profits, government relations, and self-serving interests over the well-being of users. These companies are not benevolent.
