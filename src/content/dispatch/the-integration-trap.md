# The Integration Trap

## How Congress Is Binding U.S. Military Architecture to a State Under International Indictment

On June 4, 2026, the House Armed Services Committee advanced a provision that received minimal media attention and almost no public debate. Buried on page 127 of a 505-page draft bill, Section 224—the "United States-Israel Defense Technology Cooperation Initiative"—proposes to do something unprecedented in the history of American foreign military relationships: structurally fuse the U.S. defense industrial base with that of a foreign state whose leadership is currently under international criminal indictment for war crimes and crimes against humanity.
