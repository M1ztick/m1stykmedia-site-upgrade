# **DISPATCH**

## **The Phantom Menace: Why the Trump Administration Is Inventing a Far-Left Terror Crisis**

*When the data points one way and the counterterrorism machinery points the other, the threat isn't terrorism. It's the people in power.*

---

### **I. The Three-Letter Acronym You Need to Know**

"FLT" is the administration's newest rhetorical weapon. It stands for **far-left terrorism**, and it is being deployed with the same emotional register that "WMD" carried into Iraq: a vague, expandable label designed to stampede public opinion and loosen legal constraints on state power.

In July 2026, Secretary of State Marco Rubio convened representatives from more than 60 countries for a **"Ministerial on the Resurgence of Political Terrorism."** The event focused entirely on the far left. President Trump signed **National Security Presidential Memorandum 7 (NSPM-7)**, directing federal agencies to "disrupt, identify, defund, debank, arrest, and prosecute" what Rubio called **"political terrorists"** operating inside the United States.

The administration has also designated **four foreign antifascist groups** as Foreign Terrorist Organizations, with more promised. Domestically, Trump has declared **Antifa** a "domestic terrorist organization."

There is just one problem with all of this: the threat does not match the response.

---

### **II. The Numbers Don't Support the Panic**

| Timeframe | Far-Right Fatalities | Far-Left Fatalities |
|-----------|----------------------|---------------------|
| Past decade | 112 | 13 |
| 2022–2024 | 61 (100% of political killings) | 0 |
| Since 2001 | ~75–80% of domestic terrorism deaths | ~10–15% of incidents, <5% of deaths |
| H1 2025 | 1 (killed 2 people) | 5 (killed 0 people) |

Sources: Center for Strategic and International Studies (CSIS), University of Maryland/START, PBS NewsHour analysis.

The administration's own preferred data point is a **2025 CSIS finding** that, for the first time in more than 30 years, left-wing terrorist attacks and plots outnumbered right-wing ones in the first half of the year. That is real. But it is also a **statistical blip** in a decades-long pattern of **far-right lethality**.

CSIS itself warned that the data should not be used "as an excuse for a crackdown on legitimate organizations." The administration has ignored that caution.

---

### **III. What Is Actually Being Proposed?**

The administration's 2026 counterterrorism strategy identifies three primary threats:

1. Islamist terrorism
2. Narco-terrorism
3. **"Violent left-wing extremists, including anarchists and anti-fascists"**

Rubio's State Department minilateral applies the label of **"political terrorism"** to a sweeping range of conduct, including what officials describe as **"economic sabotage including against public and private property."** That phrase is broad enough to cover broken windows, boycotts, road blockades, pipeline protests, or tampering with commercial equipment.

NSPM-7 explicitly weaponizes ideology. It flags **"anti-Americanism, anti-capitalism, and anti-Christianity"** as indicators of domestic terrorism. Those are beliefs, not criminal acts.

The strategy does not treat right-wing violence the same way. The January 6 insurrection — in which rioters beat police officers, smashed Capitol windows, and attempted to halt the certification of a presidential election — resulted in blanket pardons from Trump on his first day back in office.

---

### **IV. Why Antifa? And Why Now?**

Antifa is the perfect target for a government that wants to criminalize opposition.

- It is **decentralized** — no single leader, no headquarters, no membership roster.
- It is **ideological**, not organizational — opposition to fascism is the only common thread.
- It is **already unpopular** in conservative media, making it politically low-cost to attack.
- It is **vague enough** that the label can be stretched to cover virtually any left-wing activist, protester, donor, journalist, or nonprofit.

Former FBI Director Chris Wray and the Congressional Research Service have both confirmed that Antifa is not an organization in any conventional sense. Designating it as a terrorist entity is like designating "punk rock" or "environmentalism" as a terrorist group.

Yet that is precisely the point. A leaderless, shapeless enemy can never be fully defeated. The investigation can continue indefinitely. And anyone who overlaps with the movement's political space can be swept up.

### **V. The Double Standard: Two Justice Systems**

| Jan. 6 Defendants | Anti-ICE Protesters (Prairieland, TX) |
|---------------------|---------------------------------------|
| Charged with seditious conspiracy, assault on officers, obstruction of Congress | Charged after a protest where one officer was shot in the neck and survived |
| Convicted of beating police with flagpoles, batons, fire extinguishers, bear spray | Five co-defendants denied Antifa membership; said they were part of a reading group |
| **All received pardons or commutations from President Trump** | Sentenced to **30, 60, and up to 100 years** in federal prison |
| Several had prior convictions for rape, domestic violence, child sexual abuse material | Sentences longer than any handed down for Jan. 6 |

Sources: U.S. House Judiciary Committee Democrats, PBS NewsHour, The Atlantic.

The administration has also reportedly **fired career prosecutors** who worked on Jan. 6 cases and has explored financial compensation for participants. Meanwhile, it has used the full weight of federal prosecution against anti-ICE protesters and alleged Antifa members.

That is not neutral law enforcement. That is **political patronage and punishment**.

---

### **VI. The Real Danger: Counterterrorism as Domestic Policy**

The scariest part of the FLT framing is not the rhetoric. It is the **machinery** being unlocked.

Foreign Terrorist Organization designations and counterterrorism statutes give the government access to:

- **Financial sanctions and asset freezing**
- **Material support prosecutions**
- **Surveillance and intelligence sharing**
- **Immigration and visa denials**
- **Cross-border extradition requests**
- **Criminal penalties for donors, lawyers, trainers, and researchers**

When Antifa can include anyone who organizes a counterprotest, maintains a legal defense fund, publishes research on far-right movements, or provides know-your-rights training, those tools stop being counterterrorism. They become **weapons against lawful dissent**.

The ACLU, the Brennan Center, and multiple anonymous U.S. counterterrorism officials have warned that the administration is using foreign and domestic terrorism designations to target political opponents. Former State Department counterterrorism adviser Michael Duffin said that in nine years of work, he had **never seen left-wing extremism warrant this level of resources**.

This is consistent with history. The FBI's **COINTELPRO** program spied on, smeared, and harassed civil rights leaders, Black liberation activists, antiwar protesters, and Martin Luther King Jr. himself under the guise of countering subversion. The architecture being built today is more legalistic, but the logic is the same: label dissent as a threat, then use national security tools to neutralize it.

---

### **VII. Is There Any Legitimate Core Here?**

Yes — and it is important not to dismiss it.

There is real left-wing political violence in the United States and abroad. The **assassination of conservative activist Charlie Kirk** in September 2025, the **shooting of UnitedHealthcare CEO Brian Thompson** in December 2024, the **attack on the ICE facility in Dallas**, and European incidents like the **2024 French rail sabotage** and the **killing of Quentin Deranque** are not fabrications. Some individuals identifying with antifascist or anarchist ideology have committed serious crimes.

But a handful of violent actors do not justify treating an entire ideological quadrant as a transnational terrorist conspiracy. Doing so conflates **violence** with **opposition**, and **criminal acts** with **unpopular beliefs**. It mirrors the worst impulses of the post-9/11 era, when Muslim Americans, antiwar activists, and journalists were surveilled and prosecuted under vague terrorism authorities.

The question is not whether left-wing violence exists. The question is whether the response is **proportional, neutrally applied, and constitutionally constrained**. On every count, the Trump administration's FLT campaign fails.

---

### **VIII. Questions the Administration Has Not Answered**

1. If far-right violence has killed 112 Americans in the past decade and far-left violence has killed 13, why is the July 2026 ministerial focused exclusively on the left?
2. How does the government define membership in Antifa for prosecution, surveillance, or immigration purposes?
3. How does NSPM-7 distinguish protected First Amendment speech — such as opposing capitalism, Christianity, or American foreign policy — from prosecutable terrorism?
4. Why did the Department of Justice remove a 2024 National Institute of Justice report showing that far-right attacks continue to outpace other forms of terrorism?
5. How can the administration reconcile blanket pardons for Jan. 6 defendants with multi-decade sentences for anti-ICE protesters?

Until these questions have coherent answers, the FLT campaign cannot be taken seriously as counterterrorism. It can only be understood as **counter-opposition**.

---

### **IX. Conclusion: A Label, Not a Threat**

The United States does not have a far-left terrorism crisis. It has a political movement that has successfully branded its domestic opponents as terrorists and is now building the legal and international architecture tosuppress them.

"FLT" is not a category of crime. It is a **permission structure** — one that allows surveillance, prosecution, and financial warfare to be directed at anyone whose politics the administration finds inconvenient. The lack of a formal membership list for Antifa is not a bug in the strategy. It is the entire point.

If history is any guide, the first targets will be the most radical and least sympathetic. But once the machinery exists, it expands. The real target was never Antifa. It was the idea that ordinary people can organize against power without becoming, by definition, terrorists.
